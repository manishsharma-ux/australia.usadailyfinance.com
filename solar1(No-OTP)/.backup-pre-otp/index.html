Solar and Battery Incentives for Australian Homes

Federal certificates, state and territory programs and a smaller grid bill all pull in the same direction. What you can actually claim depends on your address, your roof and who owns the property.

Federal STC discount

Under the Small-scale Renewable Energy Scheme, your installer claims Small-scale Technology Certificates for an eligible system and takes their value off your quoted price up front. The scheme steps down each year until it closes in 2030.

State and territory programs

Victoria, New South Wales, South Australia, Queensland, Western Australia and the ACT each run their own solar and home battery offers, including rebates and interest-free loans, with their own eligibility rules.

Accredited installers only

Most incentives can only be claimed through a Clean Energy Council accredited installer. We pass enquiries to accredited installers, so the quote you receive reflects what you can genuinely claim.

Common questions

Solar and Battery Rebates, Answered

The questions we are asked most often about what is available, who qualifies and what happens after you check.

Who can get a solar rebate in Australia?

The federal Small-scale Renewable Energy Scheme is open to most property owners installing an eligible system under 100kW through a Clean Energy Council accredited installer. State and territory programs add their own tests, usually your address, whether you own the property and sometimes household income.

Is there a rebate for home batteries?

Yes. Federal support for eligible home battery installations sits alongside the solar scheme, and several states and territories run their own battery rebates or interest-free loans on top of it. What you can claim depends on where you live and what is already installed.

Can I get a solar rebate if I rent?

Rebates are claimed against the property, so they generally go to the owner. Tenants need the property owner to agree to the installation. Some state programs have specific offers for rental properties, so it is worth raising with the owner.

Does this eligibility check cost anything?

No. The check is free, there is no obligation, and completing it is not an application. We pass your details to accredited installers who can confirm what applies at your address.

Rebate programs and their eligibility rules change. Always confirm the current terms with your installer or the relevant government program before you commit.
